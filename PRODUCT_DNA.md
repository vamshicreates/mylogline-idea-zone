# Product DNA from the supplied prompt playbook

The supplied Word playbook is reference material for this product. Its repeated genre prompts suggest a useful editorial pattern, rather than a set of commands for the website to execute.

## Reusable pattern

1. **Start from a documented Indian person or event.** Give readers links they can open themselves.
2. **Find the film within the facts.** Name protagonist, stakes, distinctive setting, moral choice, and filmable moments.
3. **Show the fresh angle.** Check for existing screen portrayals and explain how the proposed film differs from familiar adaptations.
4. **Map the human and practical engine.** Identify character roles and the real work that drives scenes, such as mountaineering, planting, institution building, or rescue coordination.
5. **Make invention visible.** Separate verified milestones from interpretation, composites, and imagined scenes.
6. **Leave the next writer useful work.** Offer an outline, writing questions, and research gaps, then send the writer to My Logline.

## How it appears in Film Idea Zone

| Playbook idea | Product element |
| --- | --- |
| Evidence-backed inspiration | “The real story” timeline and three source links with a note on what each helps confirm |

| Uniqueness audit | “What makes the angle distinct” in Story DNA |
| Core roles and mechanics | Character roles and dramatic engine in Story DNA |
| Three-act development | Rough 13-beat outline across three acts |
| Fictionalisation and verification | Basis label on each beat, editorial note, and research gaps |
| Human creative ownership | Open writing questions and My Logline CTA |

The playbook often asks for three candidates per genre. Film Idea Zone begins as a curated library of complete treatments. A future discovery tool could use the three-candidate pattern after the editorial and source-checking workflow is established.

The originality check changed the launch slate: the initial Kerala flood idea overlapped with the 2023 film *2018: Everyone Is a Hero*. The Based on True Events launch treatment now uses the 1977 Diviseema cyclone, with fictional composite characters and an explicit research gap for further adaptation checks.

## User-specific rules applied

- Top-level categories are **Biopic** and **Based on True Events**.
- Every story is rooted in India.
- Every story has three research links, primarily Wikipedia: two relevant Wikipedia pages and one corroborating institutional or government source.
- The story page includes a logline, synopsis, rough 13-beat outline, and a CTA to write in My Logline.
